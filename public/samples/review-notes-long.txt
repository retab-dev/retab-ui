Review notes: the long-form text surface

These notes exist to overflow the pane. The short sample fits inside a single viewport, which leaves every scroll-dependent behavior untested; this one runs long enough that quarter, half, and full depth are all distinct geometries.

Section 1: Layout & measurement

The measurement pass predicts wrapped heights from the column width before any row mounts. When the prediction disagrees with the mounted height, the projector reconciles on the next frame and the delta is folded into the running offset.

Note 1.1 - Checked at review pass 1. The wrapped measure held across the standard column widths, and the anchor line stayed within budget through a full close-open cycle of the sidebar.
Note 1.2 - Edge case: the last viewport of the document pins to the bottom edge rather than the reading line, because the clamp permits nothing else there.
